# Phase 9: Audio + Full Offline
## Plan 09-01: Tiered Audio & Offline Verification

**Goal**: Every card speaks reliably on every platform, and the whole learning loop works in airplane mode.

### Tasks
1. **Tiered Audio Player**:
   - Create `src/utils/audio.ts` with a `playAudio` function.
   - Flow: Try fetching/playing the card's `audioUrl`. If that fails (e.g. offline and not cached), fallback to `window.speechSynthesis` (Web Speech API) with a `zh-CN` voice.
   - Handle gesture priming: Ensure audio only plays upon user interaction.
2. **Audio UI Integration**:
   - Add a replay button on the "Tone" side in `CardView.tsx` or `CardSide.tsx`.
   - Ensure audio *never* autoplays when the tone side is hidden (prevent leaking the answer).
3. **Offline Mode Verification**:
   - Verify that the app can handle a full airplane-mode study session (review cards, grade them, use handwriting).
   - Verify that service worker caches audio successfully.
