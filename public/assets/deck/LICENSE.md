# Audio Assets — License & Attribution

## Phase 2 Status

Audio files in this directory are **TTS placeholders** generated for development.
They will be replaced with native/recorded audio in Phase 7 (Admin + Deck Pipeline)
and Phase 9 (Audio + Full Offline).

## Audio Naming Convention

Files are named `{pinyin-no-tone-marks}{tone}.mp3`
- Tone numbers: 1 (flat), 2 (rising), 3 (dip-rise), 4 (falling), 5 (neutral)
- Examples: `ni3.mp3`, `hao3.mp3`, `ma5.mp3`, `zuo4.mp3`

## Planned Audio Source (Phase 7/9)

Native recordings sourced from open-licensed Chinese audio datasets.
Attribution will be updated when native recordings are added.

## Privacy

All audio is served as static files via the service worker cache.
No audio is ever uploaded from the user's device.
